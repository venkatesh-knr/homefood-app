# HomeFood: two-week family trial checklist

This is Step 7 of the build plan, the phase 1 gate: the family uses the app for real for two weeks, then we fix what hurts. It is a real-use check, not a test with a script, so the aim is to **use HomeFood instead of the old way** (WhatsApp, memory, paper) and write down every place it was slower, confusing or wrong.

Live app: https://venkatesh-knr.github.io/homefood-app/ (add it to the home screen on each phone). English and Tamil are both available from the language switch at the top.

**What "passed" looks like** (decide together on day 14):
- [ ] The week's plan was made in the app for both weeks, and people looked at it (Today or the poster) without being reminded.
- [ ] The helper or whoever cooks knew what to cook from the app (cook's sheet or Today), without a separate message.
- [ ] Nobody lost a decision or data (a meal, a vote, a shopping item went missing or changed by itself).
- [ ] Tamil was good enough to use every day.
- [ ] No one needed Venkatesh to explain a screen more than once.
- [ ] A list of fixes exists, sorted: must fix · should fix · nice to have.

---

## 0. Before day 1 (Venkatesh, about 30 minutes)

- [ ] Sign in as Admin. Home › Family and invite: everyone who lives there is added (name, birth year, sex and activity if you want better nutrition targets). Helper is added as **Helper**, not as a family member.
- [ ] Allergies are filled in for each person (Home › Family and invite › the person). Check Paati's soy allergy or any other.
- [ ] Each person who should vote or plan has a **login**: send the invite link (WhatsApp button), they sign in with the 6-digit email code and pick "which one is you". Paati and the helper do **not** need a login.
- [ ] Home › Home settings: home name, default language, snacks on or off, **week starts Monday or Sunday**.
- [ ] Assign **Planner turns** on the rota (Week › Rota): for example Amma this week, Appa next week.
- [ ] Everyone installs the app: Android Chrome menu › Install app; iPhone Safari Share › Add to Home Screen.
- [ ] Make a copy of this checklist for each person (section 3), or share the link to this file.

## 1. Week 1 routine

**Sunday (or the day before the week starts), the Planner (10 minutes)**
- [ ] Week tab: plan breakfast, lunch, snacks and dinner for the coming week. Main dish plus sides, who cooks, who is eating. Try "Copy last week" only if there was a last week.
- [ ] Notice every allergy warning that appears. Does it match reality?
- [ ] **Publish the week.** Everyone with a login gets a bell notification.
- [ ] Open Week at a glance, tap **Save as image** and send the picture to the family WhatsApp group.
- [ ] **From this week's meals** (Shopping): add the week's list. Untick what you already have. Check a few amounts: do they look sensible for your family?

**Every evening (2 minutes)**
- [ ] After 4 pm, Today shows **Tonight · for tomorrow** (batter, soaking, curd, thawing). Tick the tasks done; check "Done by" shows who.
- [ ] Around 7 pm, the cooks who have a login get a bell reminder. Did it arrive? On every phone?
- [ ] Open the **Cook's sheet** (tomorrow), choose the helper's language, and send it on WhatsApp. Did the helper understand it?

**Every day**
- [ ] Today: check the next meal. Agree or disagree on a meal; leave a comment; suggest something once.
- [ ] After meals: "Did you eat this?" (skipped, ate elsewhere, small or large portion).
- [ ] Something you ate that was not planned? **My extras** (Today, scroll down): add it. For yesterday or an earlier day, step back a day first.
- [ ] Shopping: add things as you think of them (type "milk 2 l"), tick them off in the shop, try sharing the list on WhatsApp.

**End of the week (the Planner, 5 minutes)**
- [ ] History: does the week look right? Fix a meal that changed (eaten out instead).
- [ ] My nutrition: do the numbers feel believable? (They are estimates.)

## 2. Week 2 routine

Same as week 1, with a **different Planner** (Appa or Kavi), so the app is tested by someone who did not set it up. Also:
- [ ] Add one **new dish** of your own (a family recipe): name in English and Tamil, a photo (camera or **Find a free photo online**), and a recipe if you like (Recipe › Write our recipe).
- [ ] Use **Cook this again** on a dish you liked last week.
- [ ] Change a prep reminder for your home (Dish detail › Prep reminders): switch one off that does not suit you, add one of your own.
- [ ] Try the **demo home** once (sign-in page) to see what a new relative would see.

## 4. Things to check on purpose (once during the two weeks)

- [ ] **Tamil switch**: change the language on every main screen. Note wrong or awkward Tamil (use the number in `docs/tamil-review.html`).
- [ ] **Dark mode** (Home › Appearance and tips) at night.
- [ ] **Slow or no internet**: open the app on bad signal. What still shows? What shows an error?
- [ ] **Two people at once**: two phones on the shopping list, tick different items. Do both see both changes within about 20 seconds?
- [ ] **A mistake**: delete a shopping item by accident, change a meal by mistake. Can it be fixed easily?
- [ ] **Scrolling and back**: tap through all four tabs and back. Does each screen open at the top? Does Back go where you expect?
- [ ] **Print** the Week at a glance poster or the Cook's sheet.
- [ ] **A real allergy**: plan a dish that contains something one person cannot eat. Is the warning clear? (Warnings are best-effort; always check ingredients.)
- [ ] **Recipes**: cook one recipe from the app. Are the quantities and steps right for your kitchen? Note any wrong ones (they are drafts).

## 5. Known limits (please do not report these as bugs)

- No phone push or sound for reminders: they appear as the red dot and in the bell list when the app is open.
- Nutrition is an **estimate**, not a measurement.
- Recipes and the Tamil for recipes, shopping items and prep reminders are drafts waiting for a native read.
- 30 dishes have no photo (they show a coloured letter).
- Extras can only be added up to 7 days back.
- Nobody can claim a Planner turn themselves: the Admin assigns turns.
- Shopping amounts for rice, dal and flour are suggested packs, not exact quantities.

## 6. How to report a problem (30 seconds, any time)

Write it in the family WhatsApp group or in a note, in this form:

> **Screen:** (e.g. Today / Shopping / Cook's sheet) · **Who:** (Amma / Paati…) · **What I did:** … · **What I expected:** … · **What happened:** … · **How bad:** blocked me / annoying / small

If it is about the Tamil wording, send `number: better Tamil` using the number printed in `docs/tamil-review.html`.

Screenshot if you can. Venkatesh collects these in one list and gives them to Claude Code once a week.

## 7. Day 14: the review (30 minutes together)

Ask everyone these, and write the answers down:

1. What did you use every day? What did you stop using, and why?
2. What was the best moment? What was the most annoying?
3. Was anything wrong, lost or surprising?
4. Which screen would you remove? What is missing?
5. Would you keep using it next week without being asked? (yes / maybe / no)
6. Was the Tamil good enough? Who needs a bigger text or a simpler screen?
7. Did the helper's cook's sheet save a conversation, or add one?

Then sort the fix list: **must fix** (blocks daily use or loses data), **should fix**, **nice to have**. Must-fix items are done before inviting relatives (their own homes).
